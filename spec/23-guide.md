# 23. The Guide

Feature 23 of [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md). Only Craig's answers: `/grill guide`, four rounds (18 Nov
2026).

## What it is

- The Guide is one page saying how the game works, for a new manager. The to-grill list's Help
  page is the same thing, and it is called the Guide (Craig, 18 Nov 2026). `S23.1`
- It comes in the beta, kept small, although no new features are being added (Craig, 18 Nov
  2026; 11 Nov 2026). `S23.2`
- A Guide link in the top bar, beside Feedback, opens it from every screen. `S23.3`
- Only signed-in managers see it. `S23.4`

## What it holds

- Our own mechanics (roles, runs, stars, team instructions, formations and classic sides, the
  auto team, waiver runs, "prov.") and the basics too: "yes and basics" (Craig, 18 Nov 2026). `S23.5`
- The basics are explained only in the Guide: screens and hover help never explain them. `S23.6`
- Each formation and classic side has its own entry under Tactics, showing the same held
  description as the formation window (`S05.61`). `S23.7`
- It stays general: a league's own numbers are linked to on its Rules and League settings, never
  copied in. `S23.8`
- Entries are plain text for now; pictures and diagrams come later (SPEC.md, Later). `S23.9`

## Layout

- Sections down the left and the open entry on the right, like FM's manual, with a search box on
  top (Craig, 18 Nov 2026, picked layout A of three throwaway mock-ups, with the search). `S23.10`

## Hover help

- Each hover help on our own terms (Craig, 18 Nov 2026, on #2448) and its Guide entry show the
  same held text, and the hover opens that entry. `S23.11`

## Writing it

- Claude drafts every entry, and Craig's edits win, as for the formation descriptions. `S23.12`
- The admin edits the Guide on the admin dashboard: an entry's text, and adding, removing and
  reordering entries. `S23.13`
- A section can be removed only once it holds no entries; the refusal says to move or remove its
  entries first: "Only once it's empty" (Craig, 19 Nov 2026, on #2745). `S23.14`
- An edit shows to managers at once, with no drafts or Publish: "At once" (Craig, 19 Nov 2026, on
  #2745). `S23.15`
