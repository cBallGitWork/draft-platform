# Mock-ups

The source of every board on the screens grill's canvas,
<https://claude.ai/artifact/Qz9zn3FrMHgPB189UtbjoB>, saved here so they outlive the session that
made them (16 Oct 2026). They are Design Component files (`.dc.html`) for that canvas, not app
code; `canvas.json` is its layout.

`PlateOne.dc.html` is "R3·1 · Plate, chosen", the look in `docs/design/plate.md`. The rest are the
rounds before it: A–F (`Main`, `Broadcast`, `Floodlit`, `Terminal`, `KitRoom`, `Elite`), round 2
(`Plate`, `Dugout`, `TeamSheet`) and round 3 (`PlateTwo`, `PlateThree`). Every figure in them is
made up to fill the screen.
