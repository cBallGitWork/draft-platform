# Design decisions: the history

The look's decisions that have been replaced, in whole or in part, with their dates and Craig's
words. [plate.md](plate.md) holds what binds now; this file is the record of how it got there.
Newest last.

## R3·1, the reference mock-up (16 Oct 2026; replaced 1 Nov 2026)

plate.md's values came from the designer's round-2 brief and the reviewer's pass on R3·1, and
"R3·1 · Plate, chosen" (source: `docs/design/mockups/PlateOne.dc.html`) on
<https://claude.ai/artifact/Qz9zn3FrMHgPB189UtbjoB> was the reference mock-up every screen was
drawn from. Replaced by the approved pictures in [approved/](approved/README.md), drawn on the
"Draft Manager screens" canvas <https://claude.ai/artifact/HjawmrL2GPJ3bJzT1m3Cap>, which bind
each screen's layout from 1 Nov 2026.

## Blue on neutral (Craig, 23 Oct 2026; replaced in part 31 Oct 2026)

The app was "a lot of blue on blue"; Craig likes the blue and wants it as the primary colour, on
the top bar. Of three takes, A1 ranked first in review and Craig said go ahead to its refined
board, "A1 refined" on <https://claude.ai/artifact/RSU7FzEB9rB98QnM5mTqT6>: grey page and panels,
blue bar and title plates, the plates a deeper blue than the bar. Why grey: the research on
<https://claude.ai/artifact/2FXidXSXxa1ZqmTBsqapYq> found fantasy apps and design systems keep
surfaces neutral and spend colour on meaning. The grey stays truly neutral, never slate (plate.md,
Never 3).

Replaced in part by Look B (31 Oct 2026, plate.md Decided): the bar stayed this blue, but the
plates went indigo, and the flat near-black page (`#08090b`) and solid panels (`#1c1d21`) became a
textured page under see-through panels. Look B reopened Blue on neutral: the page is no longer flat
near-black. The page stays neutral, never slate or indigo.

## The approved squad table against R3·1 (Craig, 1 Nov 2026)

28px. plate.md's rules won on the first two: no digits ("remove digits"), the page stays neutral
("ok neutral is i guess"). The row height was left to a design call: 28px, Claude's (plate.md,
