## Interaction notes

- **A team's row:** shows its squad in the middle.
- **A player's row:** opens his panel on the right.
- **Move to ▾, Move:** moves him to another team (`spec/04`: assign, move, release).
- **Release:** the danger action, with a confirm; he then goes on waivers for 24 hours (`spec/08`).
- **Free agent, Add to <team>:** assigns a free agent to the picked team.
- **Remove <manager>:** the danger action, with a confirm; the team stays (`spec/13`, Leaving). A
  team with no manager shows "Invite a manager ›" there instead, opening Invites with it picked.
  Not drawn.
