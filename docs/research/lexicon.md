## Points

| Term | Meaning | Source and quote | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| points | the fantasy score | PL how to play: "Points are awarded to players for goals, assists, sav[es]…" | use |
| scores, earns, registers | what a player does with points | PL how to play: "Players can also earn additional bonus points"; PL glossary: "registering lots of points" | use: "Saka scored 12 points", "earned 12" |
| paid, pays, was paid | — | FFS 2012: "decision to take a four point hit paid off handsomely"; FFS hits: "has more than paid for itself"; BBC: "gambled on Jacquet and it is paying off" | **avoid** for a player's points. "Paid off" is said of a decision (a hit, a transfer, a captain pick), never "he was paid 6 points". Say scored, earned, returned, delivered (FFS: "he delivered this week with a goal") or rewarded (PL: "Chris Wood (£7.0m) rewarded the 563,000+ Fantasy Premier League managers who handed him the armband") |
| return, attacking return | a goal, assist or clean sheet that brings points | PL glossary: "a player registers a goal or assist, they are deemed to have earned an "attacking return"" | use |
| haul | a big score | FFS captain: "delivering a season-defining haul of 19 points"; PL GW22: "double-figure haul" | use |
| blank, blanked | no return beyond appearance points | PL glossary: "Blanked - When a player fails to return more than the two FPL points for starting and playing 60 minutes"; FFS: "he has blanked in 17 of his 23 starts" | use |
| bonus, bonus points, BPS | 1–3 extra points from the Bonus Points System | DF points: "1-3 points are awarded to the players that receive the most BPS (Bonus Points System) in a match" | use, where the site has them |
| clean sheet | no goals conceded | DF points: "Goalkeepers and defenders keeping a clean sheet" | use |
| "he got 12" | casual speech | not checked (Reddit refused) | fine in chat; "scored 12" in copy |

## The team

| Term | Meaning | Source and quote | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| squad | every player you own | PL managing: "starting 11 from their 15-man squad" | use |
| line-up | the chosen eleven and bench | PL managing: "confirm their final line-up"; Sky: "Fernandes restored to Man Utd line-up"; FFS: "Predicted Line-ups" | **use "line-up", hyphened**. DF writes "lineup" ("Your lineup and ordered bench are saved"), the US spelling |
| starting XI, starting 11 | the starters | PL how to play: "choose a starting XI" | use |
| bench, subs, substitutes | the players who don't start | PL managing: "the outfield substitute who you have placed FIRST on your bench" | use all three; the glossary avoids "subs" (see [Against the glossary](#against-the-glossary)) |
| auto-subs | the automatic substitutions | PL replay: "Normal auto-subs and vice-captain rules will apply"; DF team: "auto subs" | use "auto-subs" |
| captain, vice-captain, armband | the doubled pick and his stand-in | PL managing: "the captain's armband passes to your vice-captain" | use; the glossary avoids "armband" for the person |
| skipper | the captain | PL how to play: "if the selected skipper does not play" | UK, but the glossary avoids it |

## Table and matches

| Term | Meaning | Source and quote | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| head-to-head, H2H | one team against another each gameweek | PL leagues: "Teams are then ranked on points earned in Head-to-Head matches" | use |
| table, league table | the ranking | PL draft H2H: "wins three points in the league table"; BBC and Sky name the page Table | **use "table"** |
| standings | the ranking | DF league formats: "regular league standings" | understood, US-flavoured: say table |
| fixture | a scheduled meeting | DF league formats: "head-to-head fixtures" | use |
| matchup | a head-to-head of two teams | on none of the pages fetched, UK or US | not UK usage on this evidence: UK draft copy says fixture, match or head-to-head |
| match, game | a meeting | BBC: "first of eight Premier League games"; PL's menu: Matches | both UK |
| gameweek, double gameweek, blank gameweek | a round; two fixtures in one; none | PL glossary: "A club will have a Double Gameweek if they have two fixtures in one Gameweek" | use; PL capitalises Gameweek, DF doesn't |
| matchday | a day of matches | not checked | rare in FPL talk; gameweek |
| draw | a level result | DF: "A draw earns each team 1 table point"; BBC: "goalless draw"; Sky table: "Pl Played W Won D Drawn L Lost" | **use**; "tie" in the UK is a cup meeting (DF cups: "the winner of tie 1") |
| play-offs, cup | a knockout after the season | DF cups: "Playoffs are a built-in top-four competition" | UK football hyphens "play-offs" (not fetched); FPL and DF also run cups |

## Against the glossary

Where the UK usage above differs from a word Craig chose. Asked on 31 Oct 2026 whether to switch
to "head-to-head" and "line-up", he kept both: "Match up / Lineup".

- **lineup**: the glossary's term; UK football and FPL write "line-up".
- **matchup**: the glossary's term (and "match" avoided for it); UK draft copy says fixture or
  head-to-head, and "matchup" is on no UK page fetched.
- **subs**: avoided for the bench; FPL and the PL use "substitute" and "auto-subs" freely.
- **skipper**: avoided; FPL's own how-to says it.
- **armband**: avoided for the person; the PL uses it for the captaincy ("handed him the
  armband"), which the glossary allows.
