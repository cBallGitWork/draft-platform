## Open rules

Undrafted players already go to free agents (nothing joins a squad, #1087) and every team's lineup is already saved when the draft ends (#1040). What's left is the new "scoring starts in gameweek N" rule, and these parts of it aren't settled:

1. **Gameweeks before N:** for a draft ending in gameweek 5 with scoring starting in gameweek 8, what happens in gameweeks 6 and 7? Do they score nothing with no head-to-head (like an unticked gameweek), and can managers pick up free agents and trade in them?
   Rec: they score nothing and have no head-to-head, the round robin starts at N, and free agency and trades stay open, so managers can fix their squads before N.
   Alt: they're locked like an unticked gameweek (no pickups, no trades) until N's deadline.

2. **When the commissioner can set it:** can he choose N before the draft, after it, or both? Example: he sets gameweek 8 in League settings, and the draft hasn't started yet.
   Rec: any time until N's deadline. Before that it can be moved to any gameweek that hasn't begun, and once N's deadline passes it's fixed.
   Alt: only before the draft starts; after that it's fixed.

3. **The draft runs late:** he sets gameweek 8, but the draft is still going at gameweek 8's deadline and finishes in gameweek 8. What happens?
   Rec: scoring starts at the next gameweek after the draft ends (gameweek 9), and the commissioner gets a Mail letter saying so.
   Alt: gameweek 8 still counts, with each team's saved lineup from the draft's end.

4. **N is an unticked gameweek:** he sets gameweek 10 to start scoring, but gameweek 10 is unticked for an international break.
   Rec: it isn't allowed: League settings offers only ticked gameweeks, and unticking N moves the start to the next ticked one.
   Alt: allowed, and scoring simply starts at the first ticked gameweek from N on.

5. **Leagues already drafted or with no draft:** our alpha league drafted before this rule existed. What's its start?
   Rec: a league with no start set scores every ticked gameweek, as it does today, so no score moves. The rule only applies once a league sets it, or when a draft ends after this ships.
   Alt: backfill each drafted league's start to the gameweek after its draft ended.
