## Open rules

#383's trade code is on main now (#1258), so this can be built once these are answered.

1. **The order changes after a pick trade**: a pick is a fixed slot, so changing the order afterwards can leave a team with one pick too many or too few. For example, with 10 teams Dan (slot 7) trades pick 3.07 to Ellie for her 5.02. Then the commissioner moves Dan to slot 2 and Sam to slot 7. Sam now sits on 3.07, which Ellie owns: Sam has 14 picks for a 15-man squad and Ellie has 16. A team joining does the same in the even rounds, which run backwards, so every slot in them moves along one.
   Rec: once a pick trade has gone through, the order locks until the draft. The commissioner's reorder and "Draw at random" are refused and say why, and no new team can join.
   Alt: the order stays open. A team holding more picks than its squad maximum has its last spare picks skipped, and a team short of picks finishes its draft with a smaller squad.

2. **Players and picks in one trade**: you said one pick for one pick. Squads are usually empty before the draft, but a commissioner can assign players first. Can Dan offer pick 3.07 plus Saka for Ellie's 5.02?
   Rec: no. An offer with a pick in it holds just one pick each way and nothing else.
   Alt: one pick each way, plus any players on either side.

3. **A pick trade still in review when the draft starts**: Ellie accepts Dan's pick swap at 7pm, the veto runs for 24 hours, and the draft starts at 8pm.
   Rec: when the draft starts, the trade closes without going through, and both managers get a letter saying why. Any pick offer still open closes the same way.
   Alt: the commissioner can't start the draft, and a set start date waits, until every pick trade's review has finished.
