## Open rules

Craig's four answers settle the trade workings. Building them, three things `spec/03` and `spec/08` don't settle:

1. **A new league's trade review**: `spec/03` lists none, a commissioner veto or a league vote, but not which a new league starts with. For example, Dan makes a league and never opens League settings: when Ellie accepts Craig's offer for Saka, does it go through at once, wait 24 hours for Dan's Block, or wait for a vote?
   Rec: a commissioner veto, which the league can change in League settings.
   Alt: none. Accepted trades go through at once until the commissioner picks a review.

2. **When the trade deadline falls**: `spec/08` says "no trades after a gameweek the commissioner picks". Dan picks gameweek 30. When do trades stop: at gameweek 30's deadline, or once gameweek 30's last match is over?
   Rec: once gameweek 30's last match is over. Open offers close then, each with a letter, and a trade already in review finishes it, as your answer 3 has for an international break.
   Alt: at gameweek 30's deadline, handled the same way.

3. **A player in an offer who leaves another way**: Craig offers Saka to Dan, then drops Saka. Or Ellie accepts a trade for Saka, and while it's in review a claim Craig made earlier names Saka as its drop.
   Rec: an open offer closes the moment one of its players leaves the squad, with a letter to both managers. A claim whose drop is held in a trade under review fails at the run, and its letter says why.
   Alt: the offer stays open, and accepting it is refused, saying who has gone. The claim waits for the next run instead of failing.
